# Optional .tech domain

No domain was purchased or claimed. After the owner claims a domain through the challenge's legitimate offer, add it to the Render service's Custom Domains settings. Copy the exact DNS records Render provides into the registrar; wait for verification and HTTPS issuance. Then update `ALLOWED_ORIGIN` to that exact HTTPS origin and rerun a synthetic caller/owner smoke test. Do not guess DNS targets or buy a paid domain as part of this build.
