# First-user kit

No real first-user interview has happened. This is a plan for the owner to conduct one, aiming for a short trial of roughly ten minutes.

Choose a small service-business owner who agrees to test with fictional caller details. Explain that the present demo is mock/text-only unless STATUS and live artifacts say otherwise. Do not imply it is ready to take real emergency calls, payments or phone-network calls.

1. Open the owner workspace privately and use **Add a business**. Paste a short business description and price list. Review the name, timezone, service price/duration, hours and cancellation policy. Verify unknown fields rather than trusting the draft.
2. Test a price question, a weekday booking, a cancellation and an out-of-scope question. Check the diary and transcript after each. Ask whether the wording feels appropriate for their customers.
3. Ask: “What part of this would save you time?”, “What would make you unwilling to trust it?”, “Would you pay for this if the missing live integrations worked?”, and “What must work before you would trial it with a real caller?” Do not lead them to a positive answer.
4. Use the feedback form after ending the call to save **yes/no/maybe** willingness to pay and the next requested feature. Record the actual response and permission to quote them. Do not invent a testimonial or a price acceptance.
5. Delete the synthetic caller data using the owner caller panel when done. Explain transcript retention and the separate Temporal opaque-ID history limit.

Suggested record (fill after the interview): date, business vertical, consent to quote, exact tasks attempted, observed failures, exact willingness-to-pay answer, conditions, next requested feature. Replace the **My First User** placeholder in the post only after this record exists.
